Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Mr Halatsan Publisher ("the App") is a personal tool used only by its owner, Mr Halatsan, to publish his own videos to his own accounts.

Data we handle

The App uses authorization tokens for the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts so it can upload videos on his behalf. Tokens are stored privately on the owner's own devices and servers.

No data from other people

The App does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other users, and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in the TikTok or Google account security settings.
